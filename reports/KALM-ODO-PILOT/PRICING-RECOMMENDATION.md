# Pricing recommendation — draft-only

## Proposed genuine KALM retail price

**R699** for the KALM Buffalo Heavyweight Tee, if and only if KALM is prepared to sell the same approved product at that normal price on its own authorised storefront after all gates pass. This is not a crossed-out reference price and is not being created solely to manufacture an OneDayOnly comparison.

## Proposed OneDayOnly customer price range

**R399–R499** is the defensible working range for review, subject to written OneDayOnly margin terms and inclusion of all currently pending costs. R449 is the illustrative case in the review workbook.

## Provisional economics

| Blank option | Known base cost (blank + embroidery) | Comment |
|---|---:|---|
| 300gsm oversized cotton | R274 incl. VAT | R189 + R85; all other costs pending |
| 240gsm oversized | R210 incl. VAT | R125 + R85; all other costs pending |
| 220gsm cotton | R190 incl. VAT | R105 product-page price + R85; 220gsm supplier price conflict must be resolved |

At a R449 ODO customer price, proceeds after a 20% / 25% / 30% / 35% ODO margin are R359.20 / R336.75 / R314.30 / R291.85. For the 300gsm option this yields a provisional pre-pending-cost contribution of R85.20 / R62.75 / R40.30 / R17.85 per unit.

The 300gsm option is economically positive in some matrix cells only before packaging, labels, personal collection fuel/time, QC/rejects, returns/credits and administration. The profitable ODO supplier-proceeds band in the reviewed price matrix is **above R274 per unit before all pending costs**; the model identifies R279.20–R399.20 as gross proceeds above that known baseline. It is not a release recommendation.

## Pricing controls

- KALM is currently recorded in NCC context as not VAT registered; the model uses VAT-inclusive purchase cost and does not assume input-VAT recovery.
- `odoWarehouseCourierCost = R0` because Munya will personally deliver to Ndabeni. Personal collection/delivery time and fuel remain separate pending costs, not free delivery claims.
- Break-even quantity shown as one unit is only the mathematical result of a no-fixed-cost, pre-pending-cost model. A definitive break-even cannot be stated until all fixed and variable pending costs are quoted.
