# Three-day fulfilment plan — conditional design only

## Before deal

- Blank stock confirmed and held at exact colour/size allocation.
- Yolanda capacity confirmed in writing; physical embroidered sample approved.
- Packaging, labels, size-level cap, invoice and PO templates ready.

## Deal day

- Cap sales at reserved colour/size quantities; maintain size-level record.
- Do not use supplier-page availability as a stock feed.

## Day 1

- Receive and verify OneDayOnly PO.
- Collect reserved blanks in Woodstock and deliver to Yolanda only under the confirmed process.

## Day 2

- Embroidery completed; inspect every garment, replace defects, pack individually, label cartons and prepare delivery note.

## Day 3

- Reconcile PO to packed quantity, issue KALM invoice and personally deliver completed order to OneDayOnly Ndabeni warehouse. Obtain signed delivery evidence.

No leg has a quoted or confirmed turnaround yet, so this is a controlled plan, not proof that the three-day SLA can be met.
