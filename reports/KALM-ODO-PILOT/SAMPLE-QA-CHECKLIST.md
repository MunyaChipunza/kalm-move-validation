# Sample QA checklist

- [ ] Correct blank option, colour and labelled size received.
- [ ] Fabric hand feel and opacity meet the intended premium positioning.
- [ ] Collar, stitching, body length and sleeve length pass inspection.
- [ ] Adult male and adult female fit captured without altering construction.
- [ ] Embroidery mark exactly matches the approved buffalo artwork.
- [ ] Embroidery is aligned, correctly scaled, stable and free from puckering.
- [ ] Thread contrast is approved on black and white garments.
- [ ] Backing is comfortable and durable.
- [ ] Wash test measurements and shrinkage percentage recorded.
- [ ] No unacceptable colour shift, seam twist or embroidery damage after wash.
- [ ] Final packaging is fit for ODO warehouse handling.
- [ ] Customer size guide and care guidance are based on recorded evidence.
