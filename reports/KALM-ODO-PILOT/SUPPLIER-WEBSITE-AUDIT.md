# Supplier website audit — 16 July 2026

## Scope and evidence standard

This audit records what was observable on public supplier pages on 16 July 2026. It does **not** confirm live stock, hold periods, trade eligibility, capacity, samples, return terms, replacement terms, VAT invoice availability, or permission to embroider and resell. Those remain written-confirmation gates.

The page does not verify current stock depth, reservation, sample availability, lead time, defect/return process, trade discount, or VAT treatment. Bulkalot is therefore **UNVERIFIED / NOT USABLE FOR PILOT COSTING** until a written response is received. An unsent enquiry draft has been saved.

## Required written confirmations before any purchase or reservation

1. Live black/white S–2XL count for 10, 25, 50 and 100 units.
2. VAT-inclusive and VAT-exclusive quotation, stock-hold mechanism, and any deposit/prepayment.
3. Physical sample availability, measurements, fibre composition, care and shrinkage guidance.
4. Same-day collection, direct-to-embroiderer handling and defective-blank replacement.
5. Written confirmation that blanks may be embroidered and resold under KALM Collective.
