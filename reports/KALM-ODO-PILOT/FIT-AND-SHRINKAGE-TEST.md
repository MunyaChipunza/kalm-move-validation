# Fit and shrinkage test protocol

No sample has yet been obtained; no final fit or care statement may be published.

1. Record flat measurements for chest, body length, sleeve opening, sleeve length, shoulder and collar before wash for every sample.
2. Photograph and label each sample by option, colour and size.
3. Wash to the verified supplier instruction only; record cycle, temperature and drying method.
4. Re-measure the same points after wash and calculate shrinkage percentage.
5. Test fit on one adult male and one adult female model without changing the garment construction.
6. Assess whether the 300gsm cut is genuinely oversized and whether the 220gsm regular cut runs small.
7. Inspect collar recovery, seam twist, colour change, opacity and embroidery stability.
8. Publish customer sizing/care guidance only after results are reviewed and approved.
