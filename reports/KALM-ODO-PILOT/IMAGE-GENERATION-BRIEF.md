# Post-sample ecommerce image brief

Do not generate final product evidence before the real embroidered sample exists.

After the sample is approved, capture real reference images for: front, back, embroidery close-up, collar/stitching, fabric texture, size label, packaging, male fit and female fit in black and white. Any supplementary ecommerce image must preserve the exact physical cut, sleeve, neckline, colour, buffalo artwork, embroidery size, placement and fit.

**Prohibited:** a different garment silhouette, changed collar/sleeve, altered buffalo mark, fabricated fabric texture, incorrect embroidery placement, Kuhle’s likeness or any private-source image.
