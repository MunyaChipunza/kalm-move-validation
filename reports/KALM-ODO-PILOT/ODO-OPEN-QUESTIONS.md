# Open OneDayOnly questions

These questions must be answered in writing before a supplier application, product sign-off or deal is considered.

1. What is the minimum apparel deal quantity and what supplier commercial margin applies?
2. What qualifies as "available stock" for the deal—written hold, prepaid allocation, or finished inventory only?
3. Is 48-hour embroidery plus final packing acceptable within the current three-working-day delivery window?
4. What apparel samples, product images, retail-price evidence and content fields are required?
5. What returns, credit, defect and response obligations currently apply to apparel suppliers?
6. What are the current Ndabeni intake, carton label, delivery-note and appointment controls?
7. Are there any current stock-freeze, payment, warranty or personal-surety conditions requiring legal review?
