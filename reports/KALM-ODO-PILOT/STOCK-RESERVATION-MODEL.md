# Stock reservation models

The public T-Shirts Cape Town process indicates that stock is checked and reserved after payment. Near-zero finished inventory is therefore not the same as unsecured blank inventory.

## Model A — written stock hold without full prepayment

**Status: CONDITIONAL.** Use only if T-Shirts Cape Town confirms a written black/white S–2XL allocation, hold period covering the deal/PO window, daily confirmation, collection procedure, defect replacement and any deposit/refund terms.

## Model B — prepaid, size-level reserved blank inventory

**Status: CONDITIONAL.** Use only after a final deal cap, approved unit economics and Munya's separate instruction to purchase. Record invoice, physical count, size allocation, custody and replacement process. Do not call blanks "available stock" to OneDayOnly until buyer requirements confirm that the reservation qualifies.

## Result

No zero-stock model is recommended. No stock is reserved, no invoice is accepted and no payment is made.
