# Conditional ODO delivery checklist

- [ ] Current OneDayOnly warehouse address and delivery appointment confirmed.
- [ ] PO matched to packed colour/size units.
- [ ] Each garment passes QC and is individually packed to current requirements.
- [ ] Cartons labelled to current ODO instruction.
- [ ] Packing list and delivery note reconcile to PO.
- [ ] KALM invoice issued only after commercially approved sign-off.
- [ ] Munya carries delivery evidence and obtains signed receipt at Ndabeni.
- [ ] Quantity, defect/replacement and returns records are retained.

This checklist must be updated against current ODO supplier instructions; it is not a historical-terms substitute.
