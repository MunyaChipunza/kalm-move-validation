# Generation Audit: Hero and Bottle Rebuild

## Method

OpenAI built-in image generation was used for a clean rebuild, never to edit a rejected V2/V3 asset. The approved buffalo artwork was supplied as a generation reference so the mark was generated as a subtle physical garment or product treatment, not composited over a finished image.

## Accepted output

| Asset group | Native accepted files | Review outcome |
| --- | ---:| --- |
| Six-person campaign hero | 3 responsive compositions | Exactly three adult men and three adult women retained in desktop, tablet and mobile compositions; no cropped cast in mobile. |
| Bottle range | 40 native files | Five product silhouettes, four colours each, front plus alternate view, all 1122 × 1402 before one WebP conversion. |

## Quality gates applied

- Full-product framing only: no enlarged detail crop is active.
- Native 4:5 bottle source dimensions: 1122 × 1402.
- Soft warm-neutral studio background, crisp product edges, intact lids, loops, handles and straws.
- Direct visual review of the full range contact sheet, the hero desktop/mobile assets and the individual native sources.
- Rejected V2/V3 files remained outside active mappings.

The generated-file hashes, source locations, public paths and converted public hashes are in `final-asset-manifest.json`.
