6. Returns

In addition to statutory rights, KALM Collective accepts eligible change-of-mind returns within 30 days of delivery.

For a change-of-mind return outside any mandatory statutory cooling-off period, the item should be:

  • unworn, unwashed and unused;
  • in its original condition;
  • returned with original tags, labels and packaging where supplied; and
  • accompanied by proof of purchase or sufficient information for us to identify the order.

For hygiene reasons, a change-of-mind return may be declined for goods that cannot reasonably be resold once opened, worn or used. This does not remove any statutory right relating to defective, unsafe, incorrectly supplied or misdescribed goods.

To start a return, contact support@kalmcollective.co.za with the order number, item and reason for the return. We will provide the return instructions and, where applicable, collection or courier details.
